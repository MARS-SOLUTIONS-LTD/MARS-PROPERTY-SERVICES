MARS Property Services

A product of MARS Solutions Limited

MARS Property Services is a real estate marketplace and rental property management platform designed for Uganda. It connects property owners, landlords, property managers, tenants, and people searching for property through one simple digital system.

The platform helps people find properties while enabling landlords and managers to organize rental operations, track payments, manage expenses, and monitor property performance.

Objectives

- Make property discovery and rental advertising easier.
- Help landlords manage properties, buildings, and rental units.
- Organize tenant registration and lease records.
- Track rent payments, receipts, and outstanding balances.
- Record property expenses and monitor cash flow.
- Enable tenants to access their own rental information.
- Coordinate maintenance requests through MARS Projects UG.
- Build a scalable property technology business in Uganda.

Core Features

1. Real Estate Marketplace

- Property listings with photographs and descriptions.
- Search and filters by location, property type, and price.
- Rental availability information.
- Property inquiries and viewing requests.
- Listing management for authorized property representatives.

2. Property Management

- Property, building, and unit registration.
- Tenant and lease management.
- Occupancy tracking.
- Rent collection records.
- Payment history and receipts.
- Expense tracking.
- Outstanding rent reports.
- Property performance summaries.

3. Tenant Portal

- View personal tenancy information.
- Check rent balances and payment history.
- Access available receipts.
- Receive relevant notices.
- Submit and track maintenance requests.

4. Manager and Caretaker Portal

- Manage assigned properties.
- Register tenants where authorized.
- Record payments and expenses.
- Update occupancy information.
- Handle tenant inquiries and maintenance requests.
- Report property activities to owners.

5. Maintenance Coordination

MARS Property Services can refer maintenance requests to MARS Projects UG. Requests, approvals, authorized costs, and completion updates should be recorded through a controlled workflow.

MARS Property Services remains responsible for property-side records, while MARS Projects UG handles the relevant service-work coordination.

6. Reporting

The platform is designed to provide reports covering rental income, outstanding rent, expenses, occupancy, payment history, and maintenance activities.

Reports must distinguish actual money received from expected rent and unpaid amounts.

How It Works

1. Register: A landlord or authorized representative creates an account and registers a property.
2. Advertise: Available units are published on the marketplace.
3. Connect: Property seekers discover listings and request information or viewings.
4. Rent: The selected tenant is registered and the agreed lease details are recorded.
5. Track: Rent payments, receipts, expenses, and outstanding balances are maintained.
6. Maintain: Property issues are recorded and referred for appropriate action.
7. Report: Owners and authorized managers review property operations and financial performance.

User Roles

- Property Owner / Landlord: Controls authorized property records and reviews performance.
- Property Manager / Caretaker: Performs assigned operational duties within permitted access.
- Tenant: Accesses their own tenancy information and relevant services.
- Property Seeker: Searches listings and makes inquiries.
- Platform Administrator: Performs authorized platform-level administration.

Access must be enforced by the backend. Users must not access properties, financial records, or personal information without authorization.

Business Model

The initial strategy is to encourage adoption by allowing landlords to list properties free of charge during the launch phase.

Potential future revenue sources include:

- Premium property listings.
- Featured advertisements.
- Landlord and property-manager subscriptions.
- Advanced reporting and management tools.
- Other appropriate property-related digital services.

Pricing and paid features will be introduced according to market demand and the company's commercial strategy.

Technology and Design Principles

The application should be:

- Mobile-first and responsive.
- Simple for ordinary users.
- Suitable for low-bandwidth environments.
- Secure and privacy-conscious.
- Designed for accurate financial records.
- Built with maintainable, scalable architecture.
- Prepared for appropriate mobile-money integrations, subject to provider approval and availability.

The final technology stack and deployment configuration must be documented according to the actual implementation.

MARS Product Ecosystem

MARS Property Services is separate from the other MARS products:

- MARS Cash Flow: Personal income allocation, budgeting, savings, investments, and financial reporting.
- MARS Property Services: Real estate listings and rental property management.
- MARS Projects UG: Maintenance and property-service coordination.

Integrations must preserve the responsibilities and access controls of each product.

Development Roadmap

1. Application foundation, database, authentication, and permissions.
2. Property listings, search, and viewing requests.
3. Property, unit, tenant, and lease management.
4. Rent records, receipts, and expense tracking.
5. Maintenance workflows and reporting.
6. Payment integrations, security testing, and production readiness.
7. Premium features and market expansion.

This roadmap describes the intended development sequence; it does not imply that all features are already implemented.

Security and Data Protection

The platform must implement secure authentication, server-side authorization, appropriate data protection, validated financial operations, audit trails, and reliable backup and recovery procedures.

Payment records must not be marked as verified until the applicable confirmation process succeeds. Sensitive tenant and financial information must remain restricted to authorized users.

The platform must follow applicable Ugandan privacy and data-protection requirements.
